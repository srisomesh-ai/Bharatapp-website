No phone number

Chat without sharing your phone number.

In BharatApp Chat your @username is your number. Give people a name or a QR code — your mobile number stays private.

Why keep your number private

Your number is personal. Once a shop, a seller on a marketplace, a tuition group or a stranger in a big group has your mobile number, it can end up in spam lists, scam calls and unknown groups.

A @username can be shared safely. Put it on your business card, a social profile or a classified ad. If you don't want to hear from someone, they can't call your phone.

How it works

1. Pick a @username

Something like @lakshmi7. Sign-up asks for a username, a password and a Gmail address to verify you — never a phone number.

2. Share it or your QR code

People find you by your @username. They send a request and you decide whether to accept.

3. Chat and call

Text, voice notes, photos, videos, files, groups, status and voice calls — all end-to-end encrypted.

Your Gmail stays hidden

It is used only to verify you and to reset your password. Nobody you chat with sees it.

Good for

Selling on OLX or Facebook Marketplace, dating, college and tuition groups, freelancers and small businesses, community groups, and anyone who doesn't want their number passed around.

Questions people ask

Can I use a messaging app without a phone number?

Yes. BharatApp Chat signs you up with a @username, a password and a Gmail address. No mobile number or SIM is needed.

Will the people I chat with see my number?

No. They only see your @username and the name and photo you choose.

Can strangers message me?

No. Someone who finds your @username has to send a request, and you choose whether to accept it.

What if I forget my password?

Use 'Forgot password' — a code is sent to the Gmail address you verified.

Is it free?

Yes. BharatApp Chat is free to use and has no ads.

Where can I use it?

On the web at bharatapp.chat on any phone or computer. The Android app is coming to Google Play.
