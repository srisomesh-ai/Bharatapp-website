Features

Everything you need to stay in touch.

Private by design, simple to use, and free. Here is what you get with BharatApp Chat.

Your @username is your number

Sign up with a username you choose. Share it or your QR code — your phone number stays private.

Private chats

Text, photos, files and voice notes, encrypted on your phone before they leave it.

Voice calls

Free, encrypted voice calls that ring full screen, even when the phone is locked.

Video callsComing soon

Face-to-face calls with the same protection.

Groups

Group photo, description and rules. Choose who can send messages and who can edit the group.

Status

Share a photo, text or voice update for as long as you choose — 1 hour to 3 days — with control over who can see it.

Live typing

See the other person’s words as they type — or switch it off in Privacy settings.

Privacy controls

Choose who sees your last seen, profile photo and status, and who can call you.

Backup to your Google Drive

Chats are backed up encrypted to your own Drive. Nobody else can read them.

Use it in your browser

Open your chats at bharatapp.chat on any computer with your username and password.

Connect by QR

Scan a QR code to connect. People need your approval before they can message you.

No ads, no tracking

We don’t sell data, show ads or follow you around the internet.
