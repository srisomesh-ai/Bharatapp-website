Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026. Kalisi App ("Kalisi", "we") is operated by Kalisi Media Private Limited, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. This policy covers the Kalisi Android app and kalisi.app.

In short: your messages are end-to-end encrypted — we cannot read them. You sign up with a @username and a Gmail address; nobody you chat with ever sees your Gmail or a phone number. We don't show ads, sell data or track you across apps.

2. Google Drive backup

The Android app can keep an automatic backup of your chats in your own Google Drive, in a private app folder that only Kalisi can see and that doesn't touch your other files. The backup is encrypted on your phone with a key that comes from your password before it is uploaded — neither Google nor Kalisi can read it. You can turn it off in Settings, and remove it from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

3. Who we share data with

  • Hosting: our servers are hosted by Hostinger.
  • Notifications: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging receives your device token and a generic alert ("@name sent you a message"), never message content.
  • Email: verification codes are sent from our own mail server.
  • Law enforcement: only when required by a valid legal order under Indian law. Because messages are end-to-end encrypted, we cannot disclose their content.

We never sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Change your name, photo and privacy settings in the app.
  • Log out of any device, or delete your account at any time.
  • Ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, or to correct it, at support@kalisi.app.

5. Security

Messages use end-to-end encryption (ECDH P-256 and AES-256-GCM). Connections to our servers use HTTPS. Your password never leaves your device; the server keeps only a slow, salted check value.

6. Children

Kalisi is not meant for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Grievance Officer

Under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it, you can contact our Grievance Officer at support@kalisi.app, Kalisi Media Private Limited, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 15 days.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app for significant changes.
