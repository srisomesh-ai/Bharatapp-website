Security

Built so that we can’t read your chats.

Security isn’t a setting you switch on. It is how BharatApp Chat works for everyone, every time.

End-to-end encrypted

Messages, photos, voice notes and calls are encrypted on your device (P-256 key exchange, AES-256-GCM). Only you and the person you talk to can read them.

Nothing kept after delivery

The server holds an encrypted message only until it reaches the other person’s devices, then deletes it. BharatApp Chat never saves your history.

No phone number

You sign up with a username. Your Gmail is used only to verify you and is never shown to anyone.

You choose who reaches you

People must send a request that you accept before they can message or call you. You can block anyone at any time.

Your account

Your account key is created from your password on your own device. We store only a check value — never your password. A new phone or browser must confirm a code sent to your Gmail the first time it signs in, and repeated wrong passwords are blocked.

Report a problem

Found a security issue? Please tell us through the Help page and we will respond quickly.
