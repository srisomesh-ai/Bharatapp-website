Disappearing messages

Messages that disappear after they're read.

Some things are meant to be seen once. BharatApp Chat lets you send view-once messages, share a status that vanishes, and never keeps your chat history on its server.

Ways to make things disappear

View-once messages

Turn on “Disappearing message” before you send. It stays covered until your friend taps it, and once they've seen it, it's gone.

Status that vanishes

Share text, a photo or a voice note as your status. It disappears when you choose — 1 hour to 3 days.

Auto-delete timer (web)

On bharatapp.chat you can set a chat to delete new messages after 6 hours, 12 hours, 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days.

No history on our server

Messages are encrypted on your phone. The server only passes them on and deletes each copy as soon as it's delivered.

An honest note

No app can stop someone from taking a photo of their screen with another phone. Send private things only to people you trust.

Questions people ask

What is a view-once message?

A message your friend can open one time. It stays covered until they tap it, and after they've seen it, it is removed.

Does BharatApp Chat keep my old messages?

No. Messages are end-to-end encrypted and each copy is deleted from the server as soon as it is delivered. Your chats live on your own devices, with an optional encrypted backup to your own Google Drive.

How long does a status stay?

You choose: from 1 hour to 3 days. Then it disappears.

Can someone screenshot a view-once message?

Any screen can be photographed with another camera, so send private things only to people you trust.

Is BharatApp Chat free?

Yes, free to use and with no ads.
