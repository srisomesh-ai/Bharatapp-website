WhatsApp alternative

A WhatsApp alternative that doesn't need your phone number.

BharatApp Chat does the everyday things you use WhatsApp for — chats, groups, calls, photos and status — but you sign up with a @username, so your mobile number stays private.

How BharatApp Chat compares

WhatsApp is a good, end-to-end encrypted app used by millions. BharatApp Chat is for people who want the same kind of messenger without giving their phone number to everyone they talk to.

Sign-up

WhatsApp: your mobile number.
BharatApp Chat: a @username and a Gmail address — no phone number.

Who can message you

WhatsApp: anyone who has your number.
BharatApp Chat: only people whose request you accept.

Encryption

Both are end-to-end encrypted. BharatApp Chat deletes each message from its server once it's delivered.

Made in

BharatApp Chat is built and run in India by BharatApp Chat Media Private Limited.

What you can do

One-to-one chats, groups with admins and rules, voice notes, photos, videos and files, forwarding, reactions, status updates, view-once messages, voice calls and group voice calls (Android app, up to 6 people), and the same account on your phone and in your browser.

Video calls are coming soon.

Moving your friends over

Share your @username or your QR code in your existing groups. Friends open bharatapp.chat, pick their own username and send you a request.

Questions people ask

Is BharatApp Chat better than WhatsApp?

It is different rather than better at everything. Both are end-to-end encrypted. BharatApp Chat's main difference is that you don't need a phone number — people reach you by @username and only after you accept their request.

Is BharatApp Chat an Indian app?

Yes. It is made and run in India by BharatApp Chat Media Private Limited, a private Indian company. It is not a government app.

Can I make calls on BharatApp Chat?

Yes, encrypted voice calls, and group voice calls with up to 6 people in the Android app. Video calls are coming soon.

Can I move my WhatsApp chats to BharatApp Chat?

Not at the moment. You start fresh and invite friends with your @username or QR code.

Is BharatApp Chat free?

Yes. It is free to use and has no ads.
